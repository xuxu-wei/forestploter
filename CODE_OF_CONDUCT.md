# Code of conduct

The `forestploter` community is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and
scientifically constructive environment.

Participants must communicate professionally, welcome questions made in good
faith, distinguish evidence from opinion, and critique work without attacking
people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption,
publication of private information, or other abusive conduct is not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
Reports should be made privately through the repository's security advisory
form. The maintainer will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and
will handle conflicts of interest transparently.

This policy applies to the repository, issue tracker, review discussions,
documentation spaces, and project-related public or private communication.
